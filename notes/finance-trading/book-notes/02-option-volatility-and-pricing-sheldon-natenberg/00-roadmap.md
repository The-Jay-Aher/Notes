## Why This Book Is in the Sequence

This book develops the working language of options: payoff, synthetics, arbitrage relationships, theoretical value, volatility, Greeks, strategy construction, and risk. It should turn an option from a cheap-looking directional ticket into a bundle of explicit exposures.

## Learning Route

```text
Contract and payoff
    → intrinsic and time value
    → arbitrage and synthetic relationships
    → theoretical pricing
    → volatility
    → Greeks
    → spreads and strategy behavior
    → position and portfolio risk
```
