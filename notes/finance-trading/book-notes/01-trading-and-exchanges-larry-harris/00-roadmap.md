## Why This Book Is in the Sequence

This book supplies the market-structure foundation beneath the other three books. Before studying option volatility or expected returns, understand who trades, why they trade, how orders interact, where liquidity comes from, how information reaches prices, and how transaction costs alter an apparently good idea.

## Learning Route

```text
Trading motives
    → order instructions
    → market structures
    → liquidity and transaction costs
    → dealers and informed traders
    → price discovery
    → market quality and regulation
    → better execution decisions
```
