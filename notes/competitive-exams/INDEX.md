# Competitive Examinations

The exam tracks remain separate because their eligibility, paper structure, syllabus, current-affairs window, and answer style differ.

| Track | Entry point | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UPSC Civil Services | [Master index](upsc/Notes/00_Master_Index.md) | NCERT foundations, official syllabus mapping, General Studies, revision, current affairs, and answer writing. |
| MPSC | [Index](mpsc/INDEX.md) | Maharashtra-specific history, geography, economy, administration, exam strategy, and revision. |
| RBI Assistant | [Index](rbi-assistant/INDEX.md) | Speed-oriented aptitude, reasoning, English, banking awareness, General Awareness, and language proficiency. |
| RBI Grade B | [Index](rbi-grade-b/INDEX.md) | ESI, Finance, Management, objective papers, descriptive English, current affairs, and regulatory awareness. |
| SEBI Grade A | [Index](sebi-grade-a/INDEX.md) | Securities markets, regulation, finance, stream-specific preparation, and descriptive work. |

RBI Assistant and RBI Grade B are deliberately independent tracks. Do not merge their plans or assume that a reference to “RBI Assistant Grade B” means both without clarification.

Version-sensitive exam patterns, dates, vacancies, cutoffs, schemes, and current-affairs facts must be verified from the latest official notification before use.
