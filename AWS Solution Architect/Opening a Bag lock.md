Try this method. It has worked for me several times with my suitcase.  
Look closely at the three number wheels. Between the digits (0–9), there is a tiny hole or notch on each wheel.  
Rotate each wheel until the tiny hole is in the same position (for example, all three holes facing downward).  
Once all three holes are aligned, keep them aligned and rotate all three wheels together by one number at a time.  
After each step, gently try to open the lock.  
**For example:**  
Start with all holes aligned and the wheels showing **000**. Try opening it.  
Then move all three wheels together to **111**. Try again.  
Then **222**, **333**, **444**, and so on until **999**.  
If this method applies to your lock, it will usually open before you have checked all ten positions.
